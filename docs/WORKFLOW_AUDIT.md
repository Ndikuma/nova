# Nova Hospitality Workflow Audit

**Audit date** 20 November 2026

Nova supports booking inquiries, reception, restaurant and bar service, cashier payments, reports, and fiscal integration for one property. The release priorities are role enforcement and agreement between the records used for room checkout, payments, cash registers, and stock.

## Release priorities

| Priority | Finding and evidence | Acceptance check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| P0 | **Generic access is too broad.** Accounts without groups get all module permissions, and the fallback staff dashboard reads global data. Users with access to the Core module can reach generic create, edit, and delete actions for team and audit records. [Role policy](../apps/core/navigation.py#L210) · [Generic authorization](../apps/core/views.py#L45) · [Staff dashboard](../apps/core/views.py#L247) | Deny operational access until a role is assigned. Apply per-action permissions and make audit history read-only for operational users. |
| P0 | **The payment form and cashier can disagree.** A generic payment of 25 saved a payment row while the invoice still showed zero paid. Generic editing and deletion also bypass the cashier service. [Generic persistence](../apps/core/views.py#L938) · [Payment posting](../apps/workspaces/services.py#L472) | Route every entry point through one payment service. Change financial records through traceable reversals or refunds and reconcile invoice, payment, order, and cash records. |
| P0 | **A stale invoice balance can accept payment twice.** Two stale copies of an invoice totaling 100 each accepted a payment of 70. Payment rows summed to 140 while the invoice showed 70 paid. The finance service also lacks an explicit payable-state check. [Finance service](../apps/finance/services.py#L5) | Lock and reread the invoice when posting, reject unpayable states, and test retries and simultaneous payments against the same balance. |
| P0 | **Cash can go to the wrong register or skip the cash ledger.** Cash saved with no open session and no cash movement. With two open sessions, a second cashier's payment posted to the first session. The code infers cash from the payment method name. [Cash posting](../apps/workspaces/services.py#L472) | Bind cash payments to an authorized register session, require an open session for cash, and classify tenders explicitly. |
| P0 | **The stock form and the stock ledger can disagree.** A sale movement of 2 saved while the stock balance stayed at 10. The generic path can still trigger fiscal preparation when OBR eBMS is enabled. [Generic persistence](../apps/core/views.py#L938) · [Stock posting](../apps/inventory/services.py#L5) · [Fiscal signal](../apps/obr_ebms/signals.py#L48) | Post all movements through the stock service, enforce one balance per product and location, and reconcile fiscal movements with the local ledger. |
| P0 | **Hotel checkout can select the wrong invoice.** A restaurant invoice matched a stay because both shared a customer and amount. The same fallback can attach that invoice during checkout preparation. [Hotel invoice lookup](../apps/workspaces/services.py#L61) | Link invoices explicitly to stays. Require review of ambiguous legacy records. |
| P1 | **Reservation validation and check-in are incomplete.** A blank departure returned HTTP 500. Reception could confirm four adults in a maintenance room with capacity two and check in that future reservation. [Reservation validation](../apps/hotel/models.py#L48) · [Check-in](../apps/workspaces/services.py#L257) | Return field errors for incomplete dates. Enforce room capacity and readiness. Define when early check-in can be overridden. |
| P1 | **Paying can close production before service.** Payment on a confirmed, unserved order marked it paid and released its table. The kitchen and service queues no longer treat that order as in progress. [Sale billing](../apps/workspaces/services.py#L150) · [Settlement](../apps/workspaces/services.py#L198) | Handle payment and fulfillment independently, or enforce the property's rule against billing before service. |
| P1 | **Fiscal status can contradict the provider response.** A mocked cancellation rejection still marked the local record cancelled. Send views show success without checking the returned sync status. Preparing a stock movement can reset a sent status. [Fiscal actions](../apps/obr_ebms/views.py#L174) · [Cancellation](../apps/obr_ebms/services.py#L478) · [Stock preparation](../apps/obr_ebms/services.py#L559) | Advance state only on an accepted result, show failures accurately, and protect already-sent records. |
| P1 | **The reservation-request handoff is manual.** Reception can reply and mark a request handled. The request has no linked reservation or recorded acceptance or refusal. [Request model](../apps/hotel/models.py#L98) · [Reception action](../apps/workspaces/views.py#L493) | Convert requests into linked reservations after checking availability again. Record the outcome and keep handled requests searchable. |
| P1 | **Production readiness needs verification.** The local deployment check reported five warnings. Browser rendering and live fiscal transport remain unverified. [Settings](../config/settings.py) · [Deployment instructions](../README.md) | Verify production settings, report-file protection, backup restoration, monitoring and real-device workflows before go-live. |

P0 covers permissions and material record integrity. P1 covers operational handoffs and errors that need resolution before relying on the workflow.

## Coverage and production readiness

- **Source inventory** The six CRUD modules expose 39 model entries. The audit traced public, staff, finance, inventory, reporting and fiscal entry points, including their create, update, transition, delete or export actions. [Module registry](../apps/core/navigation.py#L8) · [Staff routes](../apps/workspaces/urls.py) · [Report routes](../apps/reports/urls.py) · [OBR routes](../apps/obr_ebms/urls.py)
- **Automated verification** All 37 Django tests passed. Django checks and JavaScript syntax checks passed. Django reports no pending or unrecorded migrations. In-memory smoke checks rendered 61 pages, including 45 module or list pages, nine workspace or form pages and seven public or login pages. [Core tests](../apps/core/tests.py) · [Workspace tests](../apps/workspaces/tests.py) · [Report tests](../apps/reports/tests.py) · [Fiscal tests](../apps/obr_ebms/tests.py)
- **Isolated defects** The business defects with observed outcomes in the priority table were checked against synthetic data in an in-memory database. Fiscal cancellation used a mocked rejection. Permissions and reporting findings are based on source review.
- **Production configuration** The local deployment check flagged DEBUG, secure session and CSRF cookies, HTTPS redirects and HSTS. The application fixes the time zone to Africa/Abidjan and reads a fixed SQLite path despite the example environment providing a path variable. Configure these for the deployment, protect private report files, document restoration, and keep demo accounts out of production. [Settings](../config/settings.py) · [Environment example](../.env.example) · [Demo setup](../apps/core/management/commands/seed_demo.py)
- **Pending verification** Headless Chromium could not start in the sandbox. Real device rendering, production infrastructure, WhatsApp delivery and live fiscal transmission still need verification.

## Suggested implementation sequence

1. Fix role boundaries and eliminate alternate write paths that bypass ledger rules. Test invoice balances, register assignment and stock balances together with their records.
2. Connect web requests to reservations, enforce room readiness, and link invoices explicitly to stays before checkout.
3. Separate payment from fulfillment where prepayment is used, complete stock receiving and restock, and reconcile fiscal responses.
4. Verify local business data, reporting definitions, browser behavior, production settings and provider connectivity.
